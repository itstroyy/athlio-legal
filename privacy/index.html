Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Athlio is a team app for track-and-field athletes and coaches, provided by Darwin Almonte Pena ("Athlio", "we"). This policy explains what information Athlio uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information you provide

  • Account: email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider), or your Sign in with Apple or Google account identifier; your display name, optional profile photo, and the events you choose.
  • Age check: whether you are 18 or older. Your date of birth is used once to check this and is not stored.
  • Team content: teams you create or join, your role, training groups, workouts and training logs, performance records, calendar events, RSVPs, attendance, and absence requests.
  • Training videos (optional): short clips you choose to upload to AI Coach, and the feedback generated for them.
  • Messages: team channel messages, direct messages, reactions, and anything you attach (photos, files, voice messages). If you choose to share your location in a conversation, that location is sent as a message. Athlio does not track your location otherwise.
  • Support and safety: problem reports you send (including the app version and device type shown before you send), and reports or blocks you create.

How Athlio uses information

  • To run the app: show your teams, workouts, schedules, messages, and records to the people you share them with, according to your team roles and settings.
  • To send notifications you allow (for example messages, mentions, announcements, and schedule changes). We store a push notification token for your device.
  • To provide subscriptions and premium features, and to prevent misuse such as sharing one subscription across accounts.
  • To respond to support requests and keep the community safe.

Athlio does not sell your personal information, does not show ads, and does not use third-party advertising or tracking tools.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts Athlio's database, sign-in, and server functions, and stores the files you upload (profile and team photos, chat attachments, support attachments) in private storage that is accessible only through the app's access rules.
  • Apple and Google provide Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in when you choose them. Apple processes subscription payments; Athlio never receives your payment card details.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status. It receives your Athlio account identifier and purchase information from the App Store.
  • GIPHY receives your search terms when you search for GIFs in chat.
  • Expo delivers push notifications to your device.
  • AI Coach (Athlete+): only when you choose to analyze a training video and allow it in the app, the video and still frames taken from it are sent to Google Gemini, Groq, and OpenRouter (third-party AI services) to generate technique feedback. Athlio asks for your explicit permission first, and you can withdraw it anytime in AI Coach. Athlio deletes the uploaded video and frames from its servers when the analysis ends and keeps only the written feedback in your history, which you can delete. These services process the content under their own terms.

Who can see your information

Team content is visible to members of that team according to their role (for example, coaches can see attendance and training logs). Direct messages are visible only to the two participants. Coaches and team owners cannot read direct messages between other people.

Retention and deletion

Your information is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Privacy & settings → Delete account). This deletes your account and profile. Team owners must transfer teams that still have other members first. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple account settings.

Adults only

Athlio is intended only for adults aged 18 and older. When you first sign in, Athlio asks for your date of birth to confirm you are 18 or older; we keep only the result (adult or under 18), not your date of birth. Accounts that are not confirmed as 18 or older cannot create or join teams. If you believe someone under 18 is using Athlio, contact us and we will review the account.

Your choices

You can update your profile, turn notification types on or off, mute conversations, block people, choose whether to share your best marks with teammates, and delete content or your account. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information; contact us to make a request.

Changes

We will update this page if Athlio's practices change and revise the effective date.
