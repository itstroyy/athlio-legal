Help for athletes, coaches, and captains using Athlio.

Contact

Email almontepenadarwin@gmail.com, or use Help & Support in the Athlio app (Profile → Help & Support → Report a problem). Include your device type and what you were trying to do.

Common questions

How do I join my team? Open your coach's invitation link, or in Athlio tap Join a team and enter the invitation code.

How do I restore a subscription? Profile → Athlio premium → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Athlio account you used when subscribing.

How do I cancel a subscription? On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Athlio.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Privacy & settings → Delete account. Team owners must transfer teams that still have other members first.

How do I report someone? Long-press a message or open their profile and choose Report, or Block to stop direct messages.
